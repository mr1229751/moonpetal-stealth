A top-down stealth game set in a night garden: you are a small gardener in a cream apron, gathering three glowing moonpetals on each of three levels and slipping through the moon gate — without a guard's lantern cone ever filling its gaze on you. Guards patrol with pauses and head turns, cones shift from amber to red as their suspicion fills, hedges block both sight and movement, lamp pools make you easier to spot, and sprinting leaves footstep ripples the guards come to investigate.

## How to play

Serve the game locally and open it in a browser:

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```

Gather all 3 moonpetals on a level to open the moon gate, then walk through it. Getting fully spotted costs a heart and sends you back to the level start (petals you already gathered stay gathered); lose all 3 hearts and the level restarts. Crouch in shadow to stay unseen, and time your moves between patrols.

## Controls

- **WASD / arrow keys** — move
- **C or Ctrl** — crouch (slower, harder to spot)
- **Shift** — sprint (faster, but leaves noisy footstep ripples)
- **P or Esc** — pause
- **R** — restart level
- **M** — mute
- **Enter** — begin / continue
